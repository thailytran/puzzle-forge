# Inspiration bank — public-domain stories for Mystery / Adventure books

Owner 2026-11-21: books may be inspired by famous legends, classic detective tales and public-domain fairy tales
(Andersen, Grimm, Perrault…). The cover or the intro page carries a line "An adventure inspired by <work> (<year>)".

## Rules
1. **Public domain only** — in the US, works published in 1930 or earlier (as of 2026). Check the edition/translation too: a modern translation is not PD, use a PD translation or our own retelling. Pictures: never copy film/TV character designs (Disney, MGM, etc.); the written text of the book may be PD, the studio's look is not.
2. **Inspired, not copied** — new names, new characters, new places, new plot; keep only the *idea* (a mirror splinter, a worn-out slippers puzzle, a hidden cave word). No copied text.
3. **Credit line** — cover: "An adventure inspired by …"; first page: "This story is inspired by … All characters, places and puzzles here are new." (field `inspired_by` in the case frame).
4. **Kid safety** — kids' books: no murder/violence/romance (see CLAUDE.md rule 5). Dark tales (Bluebeard, Hansel & Gretel, Pied Piper) only as gentle, bloodless puzzles, or skip.
5. Puzzles stay code-built and solver-verified (golden rule 1).
