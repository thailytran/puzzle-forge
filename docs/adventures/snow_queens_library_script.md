# The Snow Queen's Library — Adventure Book 1 (script v1)

*Story · Search · Coloring — an adult cozy-fantasy adventure, 8 chapters. Book content in English; notes for the owner in Vietnamese.*

## 0. Ghi chú cho chủ shop (VI)

- Cảm hứng: truyện cổ **The Snow Queen (H. C. Andersen, 1844 — public domain)**: mảnh gương vỡ làm mọi thứ lạnh đi. Mọi tên, nhân vật, cảnh đều là **bản gốc của mình**, không dùng Frozen / Elsa / Arendelle / người tuyết biết nói / bài hát.
- Mỗi chương: **trang truyện** (tranh line-art + chữ) → **trang tìm đồ** (toàn trang line-art, cột 12 đồ vật) → **2 câu đố**: câu A cho **một chữ cái chìa khoá**, câu B cho **số ô khoá (1–8)**. Ghép 8 chữ theo số ô → từ cuối **EVERGLOW**.
- Quy tắc vàng: AI chỉ viết truyện, lời dẫn, danh sách đồ vật. **Đáp án câu đố do code sinh và solver độc lập kiểm tra** (`forge mystery check`). Các đáp án dưới đây là *ý đồ khung vụ án*; nếu code không dựng được duy nhất thì đổi từ, không sửa tay trong sách.
- Danh sách đồ vật cố ý **không chứa** từ đáp án câu đố của cùng chương.
- Giọng văn: ngôi thứ hai ("you"), ấm áp, hơi huyền bí, không bạo lực, không tình cảm lãng mạn → hợp người lớn và cả người cao tuổi (cỡ chữ lớn).

## 1. Premise (back-cover copy)

The Great Library of Aldermere has gone silent. On Midwinter night a splinter of the Looking-Glass of Frost fell into its reading lamp, and every shelf, clock and story turned to ice. You are the Restorer, sent with a single Frost Lantern. Eight rooms, eight keys, one word that can thaw it all. Read the story. Search each frozen scene. Solve the locks. Colour the library back to life.

**Promise line:** *Eight rooms. Eight keys. One word to bring the winter home.*
**Badges:** PUZZLES · SEARCH · COLORING

## 3. Setting — the Library of Aldermere

A tall stone library on a hill above a snowed-in town. Eight rooms spiral up to the **Hall of Hours** at the heart, sealed by a lock with **eight slots**. Each room hides **a key** (a letter) and **a slot number**.

## 4. Structure of every chapter

1. **Story page** — heading "Chapter N | Title", 100–130 words, one line-art vignette.
2. **Search page** — full-page line-art scene; side column "Puzzle N" lists **12 hidden objects** (icon + name); Gemini self-check marks positions; owner ticks each object.
3. **Puzzle A** — its answer's **first letter** is the key letter.
4. **Puzzle B** — its answer is a **digit 1–8**: the key's slot.
5. **Key Ledger** (end of book): 8 empty slots; reader writes the letter into its slot.

## 5. Prologue

> **Prologue | The Silent Hill**
> The snow has swallowed the road, but the library lamps still glow behind the high windows, pale and strange. Ottoline Finch meets you at the gate, breath smoking, a ring of empty ribbon on her wrist. "It began at midnight," she says. "A splinter of glass, a flash, and then… quiet. Every book is shut. Every clock has stopped." She presses a small lantern into your gloved hand. "Eight rooms. Eight keys. At the heart, the Hall of Hours waits for one word, and none of us can remember it." Above you, a snowy owl drops from the eaves and lands on the gatepost, a tiny brass key swinging from its neck. "That is Plume," Ottoline says. "He will guide you. Go gently. The cold here listens."

## 6. The eight chapters

### Chapter 1 | The Frozen Reading Hall
**Story.** The great doors open on a hush as deep as a held breath. Rows of reading desks stand frosted white, each lamp wrapped in a lace of ice. A page lies mid-turn on the nearest desk, stuck like a wing in a frozen pond. Plume lands on a reading stand and taps it with his beak. Beneath the ice you see a name card, a folded map, a pair of spectacles left behind in a hurry. The Frost Lantern flickers and the nearest lamp coughs out one warm spark. Somewhere above, a stair creaks. The library is not dead, you realise. It is only waiting to be asked.
**Hidden objects (12):** teacup, bookmark, pocket watch, mitten, quill, candle, spectacles, inkwell, snow globe, rolled scroll, brass bell, pine cone.
**Puzzle A — Word Scramble** → keyword begins with **R**. *(proposed answer: RAVEN — code verifies the scramble is unique)*
**Puzzle B — Number Lock** → digit **4**.
**Key:** letter **R**, slot **4**.

### Chapter 2 | The Map Room
**Story.** Charts of every country hang from the ceiling, stiff as sails in frozen wind. A copper globe turns once under your fingers and sheds a drift of snow. Plume hops from chart to chart until he finds a tiny hand-drawn river, still glittering where the ink has not yet frozen. It winds from the library's own hill to a place no map names. "A road with no ending," you murmur, "or a road no one has finished." You trace it with a gloved finger, and the ink warms just enough to show a hidden cross in the corner of the page.
**Hidden objects (12):** compass, spyglass, sextant, tiny sailboat, anchor, lighthouse, wax seal, pushpin, folded postcard, hot-air balloon, ink pen, brass ruler.
**Puzzle A — Letter Maze** (the path passes the letters of the keyword) → begins with **E**. *(proposed: EMERALD)*
**Puzzle B — Number Lock** → digit **1**.
**Key:** letter **E**, slot **1**.

### Chapter 3 | The Hall of Clocks
**Story.** Hundreds of clocks stand silent, their pendulums hung in mid-swing like icicles. A cuckoo has frozen halfway out of its little door, beak open on a song it never finished. Plume lands on the tallest case and clicks his talons against the glass. You press the lantern close, and one tiny hand ticks forward by a hair. Then another. A message has been left among the clocks: a line of strange letters carved in the frost, as if someone wanted it found and no one else to understand it.
**Hidden objects (12):** hourglass, cuckoo, gear, wind-up key, sundial, music box, pendulum, bell jar, calendar, pocket watch chain, tiny anvil, feather duster.
**Puzzle A — Cryptogram** (public-domain line; the highlighted word) → begins with **L**. *(proposed: LANTERN)*
**Puzzle B — Sudoku (6×6)** → digit **6**.
**Key:** letter **L**, slot **6**.

### Chapter 4 | The Winter Greenhouse
**Story.** Glass panes shine like sheets of sugar over a garden that never stopped growing. Frosted roses lean against the walls; ivy hangs in white ropes; a fountain holds one bright coin of unfrozen water at its heart. Plume sniffs a pot of tiny buds and fluffs his feathers, delighted. Among the leaves you find a gardener's notebook, its last page marked with a drawing of a flower you have never seen. Beside it hangs a tag, written in a careful hand: *Plant me where the wind is kind.*
**Hidden objects (12):** watering can, trowel, seed packet, snail, ladybird, bee skep, garden shears, clay pot, rose, tiny ladder, wheelbarrow, bird bath.
**Puzzle A — Word Search** (leftover letters read the keyword) → begins with **V**. *(proposed: VELVET)*
**Puzzle B — Number Lock** → digit **2**.
**Key:** letter **V**, slot **2**.

### Chapter 5 | The Observatory
**Story.** The great telescope points at a sky that never changed: one star, frozen above the dome, bright as a pin in dark cloth. Charts of constellations flutter on the walls like paper birds. Plume perches on the lens and tilts his head at the star. You turn a brass wheel; the dome groans and opens a hand's breadth of night. In the eyepiece, the star blinks once, and a faint line of light points to a sealed drawer beneath the desk. Inside lies a ribbon of paper covered in shifted letters.
**Hidden objects (12):** telescope, crescent moon, comet, planet model, star chart, brass lens, shooting star, ladder, notebook, lantern, tiny rocket, cloud.
**Puzzle A — Shift Cipher** → keyword begins with **W**. *(proposed: WHISPER)*
**Puzzle B — Number Lock** → digit **8**.
**Key:** letter **W**, slot **8**.

### Chapter 6 | The Tapestry & Sewing Room
**Story.** Spools of thread hang in frozen rainbows. A great loom holds a half-woven scene of the library itself, stopped mid-stitch with the needle still in place. Plume lifts a fallen thimble from the floor and sets it on your palm. You touch the loom and the threads soften. A pattern appears in the weaving: a row of snowflakes, each with a different number of arms. Beside it, a card of embroidery stitches written out for beginners, one of which is circled in red thread.
**Hidden objects (12):** thimble, needle, scissors, button, spool, pin cushion, tape measure, tiny dress form, ribbon, bobbin, sewing basket, patchwork square.
**Puzzle A — Fill-in Crossword** → keyword begins with **E**. *(proposed: EMBROIDERY)*
**Puzzle B — Number Lock** → digit **3**.
**Key:** letter **E**, slot **3**.

### Chapter 7 | The Hall of Mirrors
**Story.** Every wall is a mirror, and every mirror is clouded with frost, except one small oval that shows you only the lantern, burning steadily. Plume refuses to look at it. He sits on your shoulder, feathers tight. A glass splinter glitters at the edge of the frame, sharp as a thought you wish you had not had. Four portraits hang beside it: Ottoline, a gardener, a clockmaker and a stranger in pale-blue lace. Someone carried the shard here, and left a note about who held it last.
**Hidden objects (12):** hand mirror, picture frame, candlestick, feather boa, fan, lace glove, perfume bottle, hairbrush, tiny crown, cameo brooch, powder puff, silver tray.
**Puzzle A — Logic Grid** (who held the shard last) → begins with **G**. *(proposed answer word: GARDENER)*
**Puzzle B — Number Lock** → digit **5**.
**Key:** letter **G**, slot **5**.

### Chapter 8 | The Hall of Hours Gate
**Story.** The last stair brings you to a door of ice and silver, a lock with eight round slots set into it like a ring of moons. Seven keys glow in your lantern's light; the eighth hangs on a chain around a figure at the top of the stair, a tall woman in a coat of frosted lace, one eye clouded white. She does not move. Plume lands on her shoulder and nuzzles her cheek, and the frost on her hand cracks very slightly. "I only wanted the last story to be safe," she whispers. "I did not want it forgotten." Behind her, a book lies open on a pedestal, its title page blank.
**Hidden objects (12):** silver key, crown, snowflake, pedestal, open book, ribbon, glass shard, oil lamp, silver bell, feather quill, lace glove, star.
**Puzzle A — Fill-in Crossword** → begins with **O**. *(proposed: ORCHARD)*
**Puzzle B — Number Lock** → digit **7**.
**Key:** letter **O**, slot **7**.

## 7. Final Challenge — the Hall of Hours

Write each key letter into the slot it fits (slot number from Puzzle B):

| Slot | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| From chapter | 2 | 4 | 6 | 1 | 7 | 3 | 8 | 5 |
| Letter | E | V | E | R | G | L | O | W |

The word that opens the Hall of Hours: **EVERGLOW**.

Fair-play rule: every letter and slot comes from a puzzle answer; nothing in the final is guessable without all eight chapters; a wrong word keeps the lock shut. Printed answer pages at the back explain each chapter.

## 8. Epilogue

> **Epilogue | The Warm Page**
> You say the word aloud, and the lock turns like a slow sunrise. The Hall of Hours opens on a room full of warm gold light, and the blank book on its pedestal fills with writing as you watch. The woman in pale blue straightens, the clouded eye clearing at last. "Everglow," she says softly. "I had forgotten the last page of my own story." Ottoline's footsteps echo on the stair, then her laughter. Plume lifts the brass key from his neck and sets it in the Snow Queen's palm. Outside, snow begins to fall again, gently, and every window in Aldermere glows.
